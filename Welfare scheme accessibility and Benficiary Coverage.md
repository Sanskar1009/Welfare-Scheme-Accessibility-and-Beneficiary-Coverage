About Our Project

Welfare Scheme Accessibility and Beneficiary Coverage Analytics is a project designed to help people easily understand and access information about various welfare schemes offered by the Government of Maharashtra.
The platform provides information about government schemes, including their details, eligibility criteria, and benefits. It also includes an analytics feature to help users understand beneficiary coverage across different schemes and locations.
Our goal is to make government scheme information simple, accessible, and easy to understand so that people can identify schemes relevant to them and learn about the benefits available to eligible citizens.
